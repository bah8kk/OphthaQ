1. Who we are
OphthaQ – Ophthalmology Prep is an educational application designed to help ophthalmology trainees prepare for exams such as FRCOphth, FRCS, and ICO through MCQ practice, tutor/test/mock modes, results tracking, and related study features.

OphthaQ is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, or clinical medical advice.
